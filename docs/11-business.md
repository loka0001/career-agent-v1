# Business Model Canvas

| Block | MVP answer |
| --- | --- |
| Customer segments | Small online stores, social sellers, and small agencies managing merchant pages. |
| Value propositions | Faster product-to-post workflow; fewer invented facts; one reviewed catalog powering content and customer answers; explainable recommendations based on live stock and budget. |
| Channels | Direct outreach to social sellers, agency partnerships, merchant communities, short demos, and referrals. |
| Customer relationships | Guided onboarding, self-service daily workflow, setup support for Meta accounts, and a simple support channel. |
| Revenue streams | Monthly per-store subscription; higher plan for more products/messages; optional one-time Meta setup service. |
| Key activities | Maintain grounded prompts/rules, improve onboarding UX, support integrations, monitor publish reliability, and validate sales outcomes. |
| Key resources | Product/policy data model, workflow software, AI/vector providers, Meta/Cloudinary integration knowledge, and merchant trust. |
| Key partners | Meta, Cloudinary, AI providers, hosting platforms, and local commerce agencies. |
| Cost structure | AI inference, image storage/bandwidth, hosting/persistent disk, support, monitoring, and integration maintenance. |

## Pricing hypothesis—not a validated price

- Starter: low monthly fee for one store, a capped catalog, and deterministic/demo mode.
- Growth: real AI/publishing allowance and higher message volume.
- Setup service: one-time fee for Meta/Cloudinary connection and merchant training.

Test willingness to pay with 5–10 merchants before building billing. Measure time saved, accepted content percentage, response use, and attributable conversations rather than vanity generation counts.

## Initial go-to-market

1. Recruit five Egyptian social-commerce merchants in Audio, Accessories, Home, or Skincare.
2. Import 20 products manually and run the demo on their actual FAQ messages.
3. Compare content preparation time and response quality for two weeks.
4. Convert merchants who repeatedly use both journeys; interview churned users before adding scope.
