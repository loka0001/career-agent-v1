# Product Strategy

## Problem

Small merchants repeatedly rewrite product information for social posts and answer the same buying questions manually. Content tools can invent facts, while generic chatbots may recommend unavailable or over-budget products.

## Primary user

A small-store owner or social operator with low-to-medium technical experience who maintains a compact catalog and sells through Facebook or Instagram.

## Value proposition

Enter trusted product facts once, review AI-assisted content, publish only after explicit approval, and answer customers from current catalog and policy data.

## MVP success indicators

- First useful content preview in under three minutes for demo data.
- At least 80% completion of the onboarding-to-preview flow in usability sessions.
- Zero published drafts without a valid server-side approval hash.
- Zero out-of-stock or over-budget recommendations in the evaluation suite.
- Merchant acceptance/edit rate for generated content.
- Publish success and partial-failure rates by platform.
- Sales reply copy rate and recommendation click rate in a later analytics phase.

## Assumptions to validate

1. Merchants will review facts before activation.
2. Separate Facebook and Instagram copy saves meaningful time.
3. Three explainable recommendations are more useful than a long result list.
4. Merchants can connect professional Meta accounts or receive setup help.
